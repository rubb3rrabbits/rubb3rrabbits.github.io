The human ego is capable of extraordinary self-deception. We watch an artisan on YouTube perform a complex skill for 45 seconds and immediately conclude: *'I could do that this Saturday.'*

### The Top Offenders:
1. **Hanging Wallpaper:** A fast-track descent into madness involving mismatched seams and permanent bubbles.
2. **Trimming Your Own Bangs:** An exercise that begins with trimming half an inch and ends with looking like a medieval monk.
3. **Making Caramel Without a Recipe:** You do not have caramel. You have a ruined saucepan and an apartment full of acrid smoke.

Humility is not taught in classrooms; it is taught at 2:00 AM while scrubbing burnt sugar off ceramic cookware.
