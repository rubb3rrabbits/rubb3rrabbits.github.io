A minute-by-minute transcript of standing in front of a monochromatic canvas:

- **00:00:** Skepticism. *'It is literally just blue paint.'*
- **01:30:** Pretension. You squint, tilt your head 15 degrees, and cross your arms so nearby museum-goers think you detect nuance.
- **03:00:** Existential Crisis. *'What if the blue represents institutional solitude? Am I blue?'*
- **04:15:** Hunger. You realize you haven't eaten since 11:00 AM and wonder if the museum cafe sells pretzel dogs.
