Navigating a residential street requires split-second social calibration. Two sedans approach an obstruction. One yields. Now comes the critical test: **The Wave**.

- **The Full Palm:** Earnest, Midwestern, borderline desperate.
- **The Single-Finger Steering Wheel Lift:** The gold standard of effortless, seasoned driving competence.
- **The Left-Out-To-Dry:** When you deploy your hand and the oncoming driver stares blankly forward through tinted glass, leaving you stranded in lingering social humiliation for the next four blocks.
