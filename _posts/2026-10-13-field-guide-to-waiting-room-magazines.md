Waiting rooms exist outside standard space-time continuum rules. Nothing proves this more clearly than their reading materials.

Why is every dental clinic guaranteed to host an April 2014 issue of *Popular Mechanics* with half the front cover missing? Alongside it sits an ancient copy of *Good Housekeeping*, where every single word search and Sudoku has been filled out in aggressive red ballpoint pen by a patron who clearly had nowhere else to be.

You will read an article detailing tablet buying guides from a decade ago, and you will read it with rapt, undivided attention.
