Retail optimism is the engine of consumer capitalism. We do not purchase fitness equipment; we purchase an imaginary version of ourselves.

Three weeks later, the stationary bike does not generate cardiovascular endurance. It holds two hoodies and a damp bath towel. The doorway pull-up bar serves exclusively as a drying rack for delicates. And the oversized bedroom accent armchair? A permanent sedimentary mountain of 'clean laundry that hasn't been folded yet.'

Acceptance is the final stage of adulthood.
