A performance review for the domestic team:

- **The Refrigerator Icemaker (Needs Improvement):** Hostile and erratic. Will withhold ice for four days, then fire three cubes across the hardwood floor with projectile force during a midnight glass of water.
- **The Toaster (PIP Candidate):** Zero nuance. Acknowledges only two operational settings: slightly warm bread or bio-weapon charcoal.
- **The Cast-Iron Skillet (Exceeds Expectations):** Silent, reliable, heavy-lifting cornerstone of the household. Asks for nothing except to not be drowned in soapy water.
