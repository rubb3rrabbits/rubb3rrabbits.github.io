There is no social trap more terrifying than the preemptive departure.

You conclude your interaction on the sidewalk: *'Alright, take care! See you next week!'* You both turn your heels—only to realize you are both heading toward the exact same subway entrance, 300 yards away.

Now you must either sprint to break contact, slow your walk to a crawl while pretending to inspect a pebble, or endure four minutes of stilted, oxygen-deprived secondary small talk.
